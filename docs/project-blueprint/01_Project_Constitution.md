# HeritageAI Project Constitution

## Project Name

HeritageAI Uzbekistan

## Motto

Preserving the Past with the Intelligence of the Future.

## Vision

To become the world's leading AI-powered Digital Heritage Intelligence Platform for cultural preservation, research, tourism, and policy support.

## Mission

To preserve, analyze, and promote Uzbekistan's cultural heritage using Artificial Intelligence, GIS technologies, multilingual systems, and data-driven decision making.

## Core Principles

- AI First
- Multilingual by Design
- GIS Native
- Research Ready
- Government Ready
- Tourist Friendly
- Evidence-Based
- Scalable Architecture

## Supported Languages

- 🇺🇿 Uzbek
- 🇬🇧 English
- 🇷🇺 Russian

Future:

- 🇹🇷 Turkish
- 🇩🇪 German
- 🇫🇷 French
- 🇨🇳 Chinese

## Primary Users

1. Researchers
2. Government Institutions
3. Tourists
4. International Organizations

## Long-term Goal

Create a globally recognized digital heritage platform that supports preservation, education, tourism, and cultural diplomacy.
