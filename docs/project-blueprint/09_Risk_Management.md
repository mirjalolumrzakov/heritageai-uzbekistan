# Risk Management Strategy

## Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify, evaluate, and mitigate the potential risks that may affect the successful development and long-term sustainability of HeritageAI Uzbekistan.

---

# Technical Risks

## AI Performance

Risk:
- AI may generate inaccurate heritage analysis or recommendations.

Mitigation:
- Human expert verification
- Confidence score for every AI response
- Continuous model improvement

---

## Data Quality

Risk:
- Incomplete or outdated heritage information.

Mitigation:
- Multiple verified data sources
- Government databases
- UNESCO datasets
- Manual validation process

---

## Cybersecurity

Risk:
- Unauthorized access
- Data leakage
- API attacks

Mitigation:
- JWT Authentication
- HTTPS Encryption
- Database backup
- Daily monitoring
- Rate limiting

---

# Operational Risks

## Funding

Risk:
- Lack of financial support.

Mitigation:
- International grants
- Government partnerships
- Premium services
- Research collaborations

---

## Human Resources

Risk:
- Shortage of AI or GIS specialists.

Mitigation:
- University partnerships
- Internship programs
- Open-source contributors

---

## Legal Risks

Risk:
- Copyright issues
- Heritage ownership disputes
- Privacy regulations

Mitigation:
- Legal consultation
- Open-data policy
- Permission-based access

---

# Environmental Risks

Risk:
- Natural disasters affecting heritage sites.

Mitigation:
- Predictive AI monitoring
- Satellite imagery
- Early warning notifications

---

# Sustainability Strategy

The project is designed for long-term sustainability through:

- Government collaboration
- UNESCO partnerships
- Academic research
- International grants
- Premium analytics services
- Tourism partnerships

# Conclusion

Risk management is an integral component of HeritageAI Uzbekistan. Continuous monitoring, proactive mitigation strategies, and collaboration with stakeholders will ensure the project's reliability, resilience, and long-term success.
